Our Account Deletion Policy

At Boomers Thrive65, the official online community platform and mobile application operated by Concierges et cetera, LLC ("Company," "we," "our," or "us"), we respect your right to control your personal information and your membership within our community. This Account Deletion Policy explains how members may request the deletion of their account and what happens to their information when an account is removed.

Our goal is to provide a transparent process while maintaining the safety, security, and integrity of our online community for Baby Boomers, their families, and service providers.

Acceptance of Terms

Definitions

Throughout this Agreement:

"Boomers Thrive65" refers to the online community platform, mobile application, website, and related digital services operated by Concierges et cetera, LLC.

"Concierges et cetera, LLC" refers to the company that owns, operates, and manages Boomers Thrive65.

"Member", "User", or "You" refers to any individual who accesses or uses the Platform.

"Personal Homecare Assistance Providers" and "Home Helpers" refer to independent third-party service providers that may be introduced through the Platform.

1. Requesting Account Deletion

Members may request permanent deletion of their account at any time.

You may submit an account deletion request by:

  • Accessing the Account Settings section of your profile and selecting Delete Account; or

  • Contacting our support team using the information provided below.

For security purposes, we may require verification of your identity before processing a deletion request.

2. What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted

Once your account deletion request has been verified and processed:

  • Your access to the Platform will be permanently terminated.

  • Your profile will no longer be visible to other members.

  • You will no longer be able to access messages, connections, referrals, or account-related information.

  • Any active membership or subscription may be canceled in accordance with our Terms and Conditions.

Account deletion is generally irreversible once completed.

3. Information We Delete

Subject to legal and operational requirements, we will delete or anonymize:

  • Account profile information

  • Contact information

  • Member preferences

  • Profile photos

  • Community participation records linked to your identity

  • Service request history

  • Stored communications associated with your account

  • Other personal information no longer required for legitimate business purposes

4. Information We May Retain

Certain information may be retained after account deletion when necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations

  • Resolve disputes

  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions

  • Prevent fraud and abuse

  • Protect the security of the Platform

  • Maintain financial, accounting, or tax records

  • Address regulatory requirements

Any retained information will be secured and used only for authorized purposes.

5. Community Content

If you have posted comments, reviews, discussion posts, or other content within the community:

  • Such content may remain visible after account deletion if it is part of ongoing discussions or necessary to preserve community conversations.

  • Where feasible, personal identifiers will be removed or anonymized.

  • Content that cannot be reasonably separated from community discussions may be retained in an anonymous format.

6. Communications and Messages

After account deletion:

  • Direct messages and communications sent to other members may remain visible within recipients' accounts.

  • Personal identifying information associated with deleted accounts will be removed where technologically and legally feasible.

  • Copies of communications may remain in secure backup systems for a limited period.

7. Subscription and Billing Information

Deleting your account does not automatically entitle you to a refund for previously paid membership fees unless required by law or stated in a separate refund policy.

Financial transaction records may be retained for:

  • Accounting purposes

  • Tax compliance

  • Legal and regulatory obligations

  • Fraud prevention

8. Processing Time

We strive to process verified account deletion requests promptly.

Account deletion requests are typically completed within 30 days of verification, although certain records may remain in secure backup systems for a limited period as permitted by applicable law.

9. Rejoining the Community

Once an account is permanently deleted:

  • Deleted account information generally cannot be restored.

  • Former members who wish to return must create a new account.

  • Previous connections, messages, and account history may not be recoverable.

10. Protection of Vulnerable Users

Because Concierges et cetera, LLC, serves older adults and facilitates introductions to Personal Homecare Assistance providers and Home Helpers, we may retain limited records necessary to:

  • Investigate complaints

  • Respond to safety concerns

  • Prevent abuse or fraud

  • Comply with legal obligations

  • Protect members and their families

Such records will be maintained only as long as necessary and in accordance with applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Account Deletion Policy periodically.

When changes are made:

  • The revised policy will be posted on this page.

  • The "Last Updated" date will be modified.

  • Material changes may be communicated through email or Platform notifications.

Continued use of the Platform prior to account deletion constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
